How to

Replace a background in four steps

  1. Open the editor, add a video or photo of a person and select the clip.
  2. In AI tools › Background removal (or the clip's Background (AI) properties) switch on Remove background.
  3. Choose what replaces it: Transparent (shows the tracks below), a Colour, or Blur – a soft-focus version of the real background.
  4. For an image or video background, choose it under Image or video background and click Put behind the person. The person moves to an overlay track with your background underneath.

Ideas

  • Blur a messy room behind you in a talking-head video, like a portrait-mode camera.
  • Put yourself in front of your slides or a screen recording for tutorials.
  • Use a brand colour or a clean studio-style background for product and business videos.
  • Make a reaction video with you on top of the clip you are talking about.

Good to know

Background removal recognises people – not objects or animals. It works in the preview and in the export; exporting takes longer with it switched on, because every frame is processed on your computer. Your video is never uploaded.

FAQ

Questions about background removal

Do I need a green screen?

No. The AI recognises the person in each frame, so it works with an ordinary background. A tidy, well-lit background still gives cleaner edges.

Is video background removal free?

Yes – free, without a watermark and without signing up.

Can it remove the background behind a product or a pet?

Not yet. The background removal is trained to recognise people only.

Does it work on photos too?

Yes. Select an image clip of a person and switch on Remove background in the same way.

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. The AI model is downloaded once and runs in your browser on your own computer.
