How it works

Why is there no watermark?

Most "free" online editors stamp their logo on your video or lock high-quality export behind a subscription, because rendering videos on their servers costs them money. Cutistic works differently: the editing, the AI and the export all run on your own computer, inside the browser, using your graphics hardware. There are no server costs per video – so there is no reason to watermark it or limit it. The website is supported by advertising instead.

Your videos stay private

Because nothing is processed on a server, your footage is never uploaded. That makes Cutistic a good choice for family videos, client work and anything you would rather not hand to a cloud service. Projects are saved in your browser's storage on your computer – see projects, saving and storage.

FAQ

Questions about the free editor

Is Cutistic really free, with no watermark?

Yes. Editing, the AI tools and exporting are free. Exported videos have no watermark and we don't limit how many videos you make or how long they are (very long projects only depend on your computer's memory).

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the editor and start – there is no sign-up, no email and no login.

What quality can I export?

MP4 video with H.264 (or HEVC where your browser supports it) and AAC audio, from 480p up to 4K, at 24 to 60 frames per second. Presets are available for drafts, standard and high quality.

Are my videos uploaded to a server?

No. Your media is processed on your own device. The AI tools download their models once and then work offline.

Does it work on a phone?

Cutistic is designed for computers with a mouse and keyboard. For the best experience use the latest Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop.
