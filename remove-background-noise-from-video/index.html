How to

Remove noise in four steps

  1. Open the editor, add your video and select the clip with the speech.
  2. Open AI tools › Audio enhancement and switch on AI noise removal. Use Strength to mix back a little of the original room sound if you want it more natural.
  3. Optionally add Voice clarity (Light, Clear voice or Strong podcast sound) and Normalise speech loudness.
  4. Click Original and Enhanced to compare 8 seconds, then Apply to clip. Export your video as usual.
What it does

Three tools for a better voice

AI noise removal

A neural network (RNNoise) removes steady and changing noise – fans, air conditioning, traffic, hum and hiss – while keeping the voice.

Voice clarity

Removes rumble and boominess and adds presence, so speech is easier to understand on phone speakers and earphones.

Loudness normalisation

Brings the voice to a consistent, loud-enough level, with a limiter so it never distorts.

Nothing is lost

Apply to clip creates a cleaned copy of the audio – your original file stays in the media library. For a video clip, the clean voice is placed on an audio track in sync and the video's own sound is muted, so you can always switch back.

Several speakers at different volumes?

Use Balance all clip volumes: Cutistic measures the speech in every clip and sets their gains so all speakers sound equally loud.

More audio tools

The clip's audio settings also have volume, gain, fades, a 5-band equaliser, balance and a simple noise reduction – see the audio editing guide. To cut out the pauses in your recording, use silence removal.

FAQ

Questions about noise removal

Is the noise removal free?

Yes. AI noise removal, voice clarity and loudness normalisation are free and have no limits.

Is my audio uploaded?

No. The noise removal runs on your own computer inside the browser. Your video and audio never leave your device.

Can it remove background music?

It is made for noise – fans, traffic, hum and hiss. It is not designed to separate music from a voice, so results with music in the background vary; listen to the before/after preview first.

Will it make my voice sound robotic?

Listen to Original and Enhanced before applying. If the result sounds too processed, lower the Strength to keep some of the natural room sound.
