How to

Urdu captions in five steps

  1. Open the editor and put your video on the timeline.
  2. Open the Captions panel.
  3. Set Language to Urdu. Don't use "Detect automatically" – it can mistake Urdu for Hindi.
  4. Choose the Small model (≈ 249 MB, downloaded once). It is the most accurate for Urdu.
  5. Click Generate captions, read through the result and correct any words, then export your MP4 or download an SRT file.

Tips for the best Urdu captions

  • Clear audio helps most. Record close to the microphone and keep background music low. If there is noise, clean it first with AI noise removal.
  • Use the Small model. The smaller models are faster but make many more mistakes in Urdu.
  • Always proofread. Speech recognition makes more mistakes in Urdu than in English – especially with names and English words mixed into Urdu speech.
  • Long video? Tick Use the graphics card when available for faster transcription.
FAQ

Questions about Urdu subtitles

Can Cutistic make subtitles from Urdu speech?

Yes. Choose Urdu as the language in the Captions panel and Cutistic writes the speech as Urdu captions in Urdu script.

Can I get Roman Urdu subtitles?

The speech model writes Urdu in Urdu script. You can type or edit any caption yourself, so you can write Roman Urdu by hand, or import a Roman Urdu .srt file.

Is it free?

Yes – generating, editing and exporting Urdu subtitles is free, without a watermark and without signing up.

Why are some words wrong?

Urdu speech recognition is harder than English. Use the Small model, choose Urdu instead of automatic detection, keep the audio clear, and correct the remaining words in the captions list.
