About

Professional video editing for everyone

Cutistic is a free video editor that runs entirely in your browser. No installation, no account, no watermark – and your videos never leave your computer.

Why we built Cutistic

Making a good video shouldn't require expensive software, a powerful workstation or handing your personal footage to a cloud service. Modern browsers can now decode, render and encode video using your computer's own graphics hardware – so we built a complete editor on top of that.

Cutistic brings the tools creators actually use – a multi-track timeline, titles in any language, transitions, colour, audio mixing and export up to 4K – together with AI assistants for captions, silence removal, voice clean-up and background removal.

What makes it different

Private by design

Editing, AI processing and export run on your device. We never receive your videos.

Free, without catches

No watermark, no export limits, no paid "pro" features. The site is supported by advertising.

Made for every language

Urdu and Arabic titles flow right-to-left correctly, and captions can be generated in English, Urdu and more.

Honest technology

We use proven open-source components and credit them on our licenses page.

Who it's for

YouTubers and teachers recording talks, small businesses making product clips, students, families editing holiday videos – anyone who wants a capable editor that opens in seconds.
