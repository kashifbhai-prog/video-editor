Creative tools

Video and image clips

Select a video or image clip and the properties panel shows everything you can change about it. Most settings can also be animated over time.

Clip details

The Clip section shows the name (editable), start time (type an exact value), duration, its place on the timeline and – for videos – which part of the source file is used. The buttons at the top split the clip at the playhead, duplicate it, bring it forward or send it backward among the layers, and delete it.

Entrance and exit animations

In Animation, pick how the clip appears – Fade in, Slide in, Zoom in, Pop up (and Typewriter for text) – and how it leaves – Fade out, Slide out, Zoom out – each with its own duration. These are the quickest way to make titles and stickers look polished.

Speed and reverse

In Speed, drag the slider or pick a preset from 0.25× (slow motion) to 4× (fast). The clip's length on the timeline changes accordingly and its sound keeps its natural pitch. For video clips, Reverse plays the clip backwards (reversed clips are silent).

Keyframe animation

Keyframes let a value change over time – for example a slow zoom, a logo sliding in, or music fading under speech.

  1. Move the playhead to where the animation should start.
  2. Click the small ◇ diamond next to a setting (Position X/Y, Scale, Rotation, Opacity or Volume) to add a keyframe.
  3. Move the playhead later and change the value – a second keyframe is added automatically.

The value now moves smoothly between the keyframes. The Keyframes section lists them all: change a keyframe's time, its easing (Linear, Ease in, Ease out, Ease in-out) or delete it. The arrows beside a slider jump to the previous or next keyframe, and keyframes appear as small diamonds on the clip in the timeline – drag them to retime.
