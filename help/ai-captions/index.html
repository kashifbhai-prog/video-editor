Auto captions and subtitles

Cutistic listens to the speech in your video and writes captions that appear exactly with the voice. Captions are ordinary text you can correct and style – and they are burned into your exported video.

Generate captions

  1. Put your clips on the timeline and open the Captions panel.
  2. Choose the Audio to listen to: the whole timeline, or only the selected clip(s) (useful when music plays on another track).
  3. Choose the Language: Detect automatically, English, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic or Punjabi.
  4. Choose the Model (see below) and click Generate captions.

A progress bar shows the download (first time only) and the transcription, with an estimate of the time left. You can keep editing meanwhile, or click Cancel. When done, the captions appear on a Captions text track.

The model runs on your computer – your audio is never uploaded. Tick Use the graphics card when available for faster transcription of long videos (the download is larger).

Tips for Urdu

Select Urdu as the language instead of "Detect automatically" (which can mistake Urdu for Hindi), use the Small model, and check the result – the speech model makes more mistakes in Urdu than in English. Captions in Urdu use the Noto Nastaliq Urdu font automatically.

Editing captions

The Edit captions list shows every caption; the one under the playhead is highlighted. For each caption you can:

  • Click its time to jump there.
  • Change the start and end times (captions on the same track never overlap).
  • Correct the text – press Enter for a new line.
  • Delete it with the bin icon.

Add creates a new caption at the playhead. Because captions are text clips, you can also move and trim them on the timeline like any clip.

Styling all captions

The Style section changes every caption at once: font, size, text colour, background box (with transparency), outline, and position (top, middle, bottom or any height). Show captions hides or shows the caption track in the preview and the export.

If all captions are slightly early or late, use Sync −0.1 s / +0.1 s to shift them together.

SRT subtitle files

  • SRT (download) saves the captions as an .srt file for YouTube, Facebook or a video player.
  • Import loads an .srt file as captions on the timeline.

How good are the captions?

For clear English speech the captions are usually word-perfect and appear within a fraction of a second of the voice. Background music, several people talking at once, strong accents and less common languages reduce the accuracy – so always read through the captions before you publish.
