Audio

Audio editing

Every clip with sound – audio files and the sound of video clips – has its own audio settings. What you hear in the editor is exactly what is exported.

Adding music and sounds

Import audio files in the Media or Audio panel and drag them onto an audio track (or click + to place them at the playhead). Audio clips show their waveform, so you can see where the loud parts are.

The audio settings

Select a clip with sound and open the Audio section in its properties.

SettingWhat it does
Clip volume0–100%. Can be animated with keyframes (◇), for example to lower music while someone speaks.
GainBoosts or lowers the level by up to ±24 dB – for clips that are much too quiet or loud.
NormalizeSets the gain so the loudest moment reaches −1 dB, without clipping.
Fade in / Fade outSmooth start and end. Fades are drawn as ramps on the clip in the timeline.
Bass / TrebleWarmer or brighter sound (±15 dB).
EqualizerFive bands – 60 Hz, 250 Hz, 1 kHz, 4 kHz and 12 kHz (±12 dB each).
BalanceMoves the sound to the left or right speaker.
Noise reductionRemoves rumble and the hiss or hum between words. For stronger cleaning use AI voice enhancement.
Reset audioPuts every audio setting of the clip back to neutral.

Track volume (select a track header) changes the level of a whole track from 0 to 200% – handy for turning all the music down at once. The speaker icon on a track header mutes the track.

Extracting audio from a video

To edit a video's sound separately – for example to keep talking while cutting the picture – select the video clip and click Extract audio in its Audio section, or right-click the clip and choose Extract audio. The sound is placed on an audio track at exactly the same time and the video clip is muted. Undo brings everything back.

Recording a voice-over

  1. Open the Audio panel and put the playhead where the narration should start.
  2. Leave Play the timeline while recording ticked if you want to speak over the picture (use headphones to avoid an echo).
  3. Click Record voice-over. The first time, your browser asks for permission to use the microphone – allow it.
  4. Watch the level meter while you speak, then click Stop. The recording is placed on an audio track at the playhead. The ✕ button discards a take.
No microphone access?

If you blocked the microphone, click the lock icon in your browser's address bar, allow the microphone for this site and try again.

Balancing levels

Music should usually sit well below speech. Lower the music clip or track volume, or use AI tools › Audio enhancement › Balance all clip volumes to bring every clip to the same speech loudness automatically.
