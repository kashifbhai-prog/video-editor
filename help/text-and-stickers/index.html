Creative tools

Text, titles and stickers

Add titles, labels and lower thirds in any language, and decorate your video with stickers or your own logos and images.

Adding text

  1. Open the Text panel from the left rail.
  2. Click a style: Title, Subtitle, Outline, Drop shadow, Label box, Lower third, Arabic or Urdu. The text appears at the playhead on a text track.
  3. Type your words in the text box of the properties panel – or double-click the text in the player and type directly.

Text clips can be moved, scaled, rotated and animated like any other clip – see Video and image clips. The Typewriter entrance reveals the text letter by letter.

Urdu and Arabic

Urdu and Arabic text is laid out right-to-left automatically, even mixed with English words. Use the Urdu or Arabic text style, or pick the Noto Nastaliq Urdu or Noto Naskh Arabic font for the most authentic letter shapes.

Stickers and your own images

Open the Stickers panel:

  • Click a built-in sticker to place it at the playhead on an overlay track, or drag it onto any visual track.
  • Upload your own PNG, WebP or SVG images (logos, watermarks, icons). Transparency is kept.

Move, scale and rotate stickers by dragging them in the player, and set how long they stay by trimming the clip.

Captions

For subtitles that follow speech, don't type them by hand – use Auto captions.
