Creative tools

Colour, filters, effects and transitions

Give your video a look: correct its colours, apply a filter, add effects and blend clips together with transitions. Everything renders on your graphics card in real time.

Colour correction

Select a clip and open the Colour section in its properties. Each slider starts at neutral; the reset arrow next to a slider brings it back.

SettingUse it to…
Brightness / Exposuremake the picture lighter or darker.
Contrastincrease or soften the difference between light and dark.
Saturationmake colours stronger, or remove them for black and white.
Temperature / Tintwarm up (orange) or cool down (blue) the picture, and fix green or magenta casts.
Highlights / Shadowsrecover bright skies or lift dark areas.
Sharpnessmake details crisper.
Fade / Vignettea washed-out film look, or darker edges that draw the eye to the centre.

Filters

Open the Filters panel from the left rail. Select one or more clips and click a filter – or drag a filter onto a clip. Each filter has an Intensity slider in the clip's properties.

Available looks: Golden Hour, Terracotta, Arctic, Harbor, Graphite, Nightfall, Sepia Press, Faded Reel, Pastel Day, Pop Max and Lush.

Effects

Open the Effects panel. With a clip selected, click an effect to add it – or drag it onto any clip. A clip can have several effects; in its properties you can change each effect's intensity, switch it off temporarily, change the order or remove it.

Transitions

Transitions blend from one clip into the next. The two clips must touch on the same track.

  1. Open the Transitions panel (hover a tile to preview it).
  2. Drag a transition onto the cut between two clips – or select a clip and click a transition to apply it to its cut.
  3. Click the transition marker on the timeline to change its type or duration; press Delete to remove it.
GroupTransitions
BasicFade (through black), Dissolve, Crossfade
WipeWipe Left, Wipe Right, Wipe Up, Wipe Down
MotionZoom In, Zoom Out, Slide Left, Slide Right
BlurBlur

The sound of the two clips crossfades during the transition automatically.

You can put a transition on every cut – including the split points of a single video. At a split point both sides would show the very same frames, so Cutistic freezes the outgoing clip on its frame while the video plays on underneath; the wipe, slide or fade is then clearly visible and the sound continues without a break. (On a split still image both sides are the same picture, so a transition there has nothing to reveal.)
