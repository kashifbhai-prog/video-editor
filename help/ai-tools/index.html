AI tools

AI tools

The AI tools panel holds five assistants that do tedious editing for you. They all run on your computer, are free to use without limits, and every change they make is a normal edit you can undo.

Before you start

Each tool shows a progress bar with the time left and a Cancel button. Tools that need a model download it once (you will see "Downloading model"), after which they also work offline.

Silence removal

Cut out the pauses in talking-head videos, tutorials and podcasts.

  1. Open AI tools › Silence removal and click Detect silence.
  2. The pauses appear as red bands across the timeline and in a list with their times and lengths.
  3. Review them: untick a pause in the list – or click the top of its band on the timeline – to keep it (it turns grey). Click a time to jump there.
  4. Click Remove … pauses.

The pauses are cut from all unlocked tracks at once and the gaps close, so the picture, the voice, the music and the captions stay in sync. The whole removal is one undo step.

SettingMeaning
Automatic levelCutistic measures your recording's background noise and speech and sets the silence level itself. Untick to choose a level in dB.
Shortest pausePauses shorter than this are kept (natural breaths).
Keep around speechA little silence left before and after each cut so speech doesn't sound chopped.

Changing these settings updates the result instantly, without detecting again.

Audio enhancement

Make recordings from a phone or laptop microphone sound clean and clear. Select one clip with speech, then choose:

  • AI noise removal – a neural network (RNNoise) removes fan, traffic, hum and hiss, even underneath the voice. Strength mixes back some of the original for a more natural room sound.
  • Voice clarity – Light, Clear voice or Strong (podcast): removes rumble and boominess and adds presence.
  • Normalise speech loudness – brings the voice to a consistent, loud-enough level, with a limiter so it never distorts.

Click Original and Enhanced to compare 8 seconds (from the playhead) before you decide. Apply to clip creates a cleaned copy of the audio (the original file stays in your media library). For a video clip, the clean voice is placed on an audio track in sync and the video's own sound is muted.

Balance all clip volumes measures every clip's speech and sets their gains so all speakers sound equally loud.

Smart cuts

Let Cutistic watch a long clip and suggest where to cut. Select a video clip and click Analyse selected clip. It finds:

SuggestionWhat it means
Scene changeThe picture changes completely – split here to edit the scenes separately (shown as dashed blue lines).
Repeated takeA scene that looks like a later one – usually a take you redid. Suggests removing the earlier one.
Long pauseNobody speaks for a while.
Nothing happensThe picture is frozen and silent.
Dark footage / BlurryBlack frames, or a stretch that is out of focus compared with the rest of the scene.

Suggested cuts are shown as amber bands on the timeline. Accept (✓) or reject (✕) each one, or use Apply all cuts / Split at scenes. Accepted cuts close the gap on all tracks, just like silence removal.

Background removal

Remove the background behind a person – no green screen needed.

  1. Select a video or image clip showing a person.
  2. In AI tools › Background removal (or the clip's Background (AI) properties) switch on Remove background.
  3. Choose what replaces it: Transparent (shows the tracks below), Colour or Blur (a soft-focus version of the real background).

For an image or video background, choose it under Image or video background and click Put behind the person – the person moves to an overlay track and your background is placed underneath for the same time.

  • Quality model (≈ 16 MB) gives the cleanest edges and hair; Fast (≈ 0.25 MB) is quicker but rougher.
  • Edge threshold and Edge softness fine-tune the cut-out.
Good to know

Background removal recognises people, not objects or animals. It works in the preview and in the export; exporting is slower with it switched on because every frame is processed.

Summary and highlights

Turn a long talk into a short clip for social media.

  1. Choose the Highlight length (10, 15, 30 or 60 seconds) and click Summarise video. If there are no captions yet, the speech is transcribed first.
  2. You get a summary (the most important sentences of the video), keywords, important moments with timestamps (click to jump) and a list of highlights.
  3. Untick any highlight you don't want and click Insert highlight reel.

The reel is added after the end of your edit as normal clips – picture, sound and captions together – so you can trim it further or export just that part.
