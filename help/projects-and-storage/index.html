Projects, saving and storage

Vyora has no cloud account: your project and its media are stored in your browser on this computer. This keeps your files private – and means a few things are worth knowing.

Saving

  • Press Ctrl + S or click the save icon in the top bar.
  • The top bar shows Unsaved changes, Saving… or when the project was last saved.
  • Your imported media files are stored together with the project, so they are there next time.

Autosave

Open Settings (gear icon) and choose Autosave: Off, Every minute or Every 5 minutes. If you try to close the tab with unsaved changes, the browser asks you first.

Opening the editor again

Open the editor in the same browser on the same computer and your last saved project is restored automatically, media included.

Starting a new project

In Settings › Danger zone, New project clears the current project, its saved copy and its stored media. Export anything you want to keep first.

Important: where your work lives

Your project is only in this browser

It is not on our servers and does not sync between computers or browsers. Clearing your browser's "site data" / "cookies and site data", or using a private window, removes it.

  • Always export finished videos – the MP4 is yours to keep anywhere.
  • Browsers limit how much a website may store. Very large media libraries may not fit; the editor tells you if media could not be stored.
  • The Project file (.json) in the export dialog is a backup of your project's structure and settings (it does not contain the media files).
