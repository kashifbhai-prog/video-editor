Editing

The player (preview)

The player in the middle of the editor shows your edit exactly as it will be exported – titles, effects, transitions, captions and all.

Drag the bar above the buttons to scrub through the video. The time on the left is the current position and the total length, shown as hours:minutes:seconds:frames.

Preview quality

If playback stutters on a slower computer, set the quality menu from Full quality to 1/2 or 1/4 quality. This only affects the preview – exports are always rendered at full quality.

Aspect ratio

The ratio menu (16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5) changes the canvas shape of your project. Choose it at the start: 16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for Reels, Shorts and TikTok, 1:1 or 4:5 for feed posts. Use Fit canvas / Fill canvas on a clip to adapt it after changing the ratio.

Editing in the picture

Select an image, video, sticker or text clip and its frame appears in the player:

  • Drag inside the frame to move it.
  • Drag a corner handle to scale it.
  • Drag the round handle above to rotate it – hold Shift to rotate in 15° steps.
  • Double-click a text to type directly in the picture; press Ctrl + Enter (or click elsewhere) to finish.

Previewing a library item

Clicking an item in the Media panel shows it in the player on its own (the label "Source:" appears above the player). Click Timeline above the player – or select any clip – to return to your edit.
