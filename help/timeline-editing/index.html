Editing

Editing on the timeline

The timeline at the bottom of the editor is where your video is built: clips laid out on tracks, from left (start) to right (end). Here is everything you can do with them.

The playhead and the ruler

The blue line is the playhead – the moment shown in the player. Click or drag in the ruler (or on an empty part of a track) to move it. The time at the top of the timeline shows its exact position in hours:minutes:seconds:frames.

Selecting clips

  • Click a clip to select it. Its settings appear in the properties panel.
  • Ctrl + click (or Shift + click) adds or removes clips from the selection.
  • Ctrl + A selects every clip; Esc clears the selection.

Moving clips

Drag a clip left or right to change when it plays, or up and down to move it to another track of the same kind (video and overlay tracks accept pictures, audio tracks accept sound). Several selected clips move together. Clips never overlap on a track – a clip dropped where there is no room is placed in the nearest free spot.

With snapping on (magnet button in the timeline toolbar), clips stick to the playhead and to the edges of other clips, shown by a yellow guide line.

Trimming

Move the mouse to the left or right edge of a clip until the cursor shows ↔, then drag:

  • Left edge: changes where the clip starts (cuts off or brings back the beginning).
  • Right edge: changes where it ends.

Trimming never goes past the start or end of the original file, never overlaps the neighbouring clip and keeps every clip at least 0.1 seconds long. Images and text can be stretched to any length.

Faster: put the playhead where you want the cut and press Q to trim the clip's start to it, or W to trim its end to it.

Splitting

Place the playhead inside a clip, select it and press S (or the scissors button). The clip becomes two independent clips at that frame. Keyframes, entrance / exit animations and audio fades are split sensibly: the first part keeps the entrance, the second the exit.

Copy, cut, paste, duplicate and delete

ActionShortcutWhat happens
CopyCtrl + CCopies the selected clips.
CutCtrl + XCopies them and removes them from the timeline.
PasteCtrl + VPastes at the playhead, on the clips' original tracks, keeping their spacing.
DuplicateCtrl + DPlaces a copy right after each selected clip.
DeleteDelete / BackspaceRemoves the selected clips.

The right-click menu

Right-click any clip for a menu with everything that applies to it. The clip is selected first; if it is already part of a multi-selection, the whole selection is kept.

  • Cut, Copy, Paste at playhead, Duplicate, Delete – as above.
  • Split at playhead, Trim start / end to playhead – available when the playhead is inside the clip.
  • Extract audio (video clips) – puts the video's sound on its own audio clip.
  • Speed submenu – 0.25× to 4×.
  • Arrange submenu – bring an image or video forward or send it backward among the layers.
  • Rename…, Lock / Unlock track, Mute / Unmute track.
  • Show properties and Select all.

For audio, image and text clips the menu also shows their key details (duration, start, volume, size, font and so on). Unavailable actions are greyed out – hover them to see why. Use the arrow keys and Enter to choose with the keyboard, and Esc to close.

Zooming and scrolling

  • Zoom with the slider at the right of the timeline toolbar, the = / - keys, or Ctrl + mouse wheel over the timeline.
  • Zoom to fit shows the whole edit.
  • Scroll sideways with the scrollbar or Shift + mouse wheel.

Undo and redo

Ctrl + Z undoes the last change, Ctrl + Y (or Ctrl + Shift + Z) redoes it. Every edit can be undone – including AI edits such as silence removal or inserted highlights, which count as a single step.
