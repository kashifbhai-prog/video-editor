Editing

Tracks and layers

Tracks are the horizontal rows of the timeline. Each holds one kind of content, and tracks higher up are drawn on top of the ones below.

Kinds of tracks

TrackHoldsTypical use
TextText clipsTitles, captions, lower thirds. Always on top.
OverlayImages and videosLogos, stickers, picture-in-picture, a person with the background removed.
Video (main)Images and videosYour main footage. The main video track cannot be deleted.
AudioAudio filesMusic, voice-over, sound effects, extracted or enhanced audio.

A new project starts with one track of each kind. The sound of video clips plays from the video track itself – you don't need an audio track for it.

Adding and removing tracks

  • Click + Track in the timeline toolbar or Add track below the last track and choose the kind.
  • Or drop a media file on the empty row below the tracks – a matching track is created.
  • To remove a track, click its header to select it and press Delete. Its clips are removed with it (undo brings them back).

Track controls

Each track header has:

  • Name – double-click to rename.
  • Eye – hide or show the track in the player and in the export.
  • Speaker – mute or unmute the track's sound (also in the export).
  • Lock – protect the track. Locked clips cannot be moved, trimmed, split or deleted; AI edits such as silence removal leave locked tracks alone.

Click a track header to select the track. Its properties then include Track volume (0–200%), which applies on top of each clip's own volume.

Layer order

Tracks higher in the list are drawn in front. To put an image above another, move it to a higher overlay track: drag the clip, or right-click it and use Arrange › Bring forward / Send backward. Tracks of the same kind can also be moved up or down from the track properties.

Picture-in-picture

Put your main video on the video track and a second video on an overlay track, then scale it down and move it into a corner by dragging it in the player.
