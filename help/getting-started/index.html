Basics

Getting started

Cutistic is a full video editor that runs in your web browser. This guide shows you around the screen and takes you from your first import to your first exported video.

What you need

  • A computer with a recent browser. Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (version 94 or newer) works best. Safari 16.4+ and Firefox 130+ have the required technology but have not been fully tested.
  • No account and no installation. Open the editor and start.
  • An internet connection only for the first use of AI tools. They download their models once; editing and export work offline.
Your files stay with you

Everything you import is processed inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded to Cutistic's servers.

Import your media

  1. Open the Media panel (first icon in the left rail).
  2. Click Import, press Ctrl + I, or drag files from your computer onto the editor.
  3. Wait for the thumbnails to appear. Videos show their length, images their size and audio files a waveform.

Supported files (up to 4 GB each):

TypeFormats
VideoMP4, M4V, MOV, WebM, MKV, OGV (playback depends on the codecs your browser supports)
ImagePNG, JPG / JPEG, GIF, WebP, BMP, AVIF, SVG
AudioMP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG, OGA, FLAC, Opus, WebA

In the Media panel you can search by name, filter by type (All, Video, Images, Audio) and switch between grid and list view. Click an item to preview it in the player; click Timeline above the player to go back to your edit.

Put clips on the timeline

  • Drag a media item onto a track at the time you want.
  • Or hover it and click +: videos and images are added to the end of the main track, audio is added at the playhead on an audio track.
  • Dropping on the empty row below the tracks creates a new track that fits the media.

Click a clip to select it – its settings open on the right. Learn everything about moving, trimming and splitting in Editing on the timeline.

Set up your project

With nothing selected, the Project tab on the right shows the canvas settings:

  • Aspect ratio: 16:9 (YouTube, landscape), 9:16 (Reels, Shorts, TikTok), 1:1 (square) or 4:5 (portrait feed posts).
  • Resolution: the editing canvas size (720p to 4K).
  • Frame rate: 24, 25, 30, 50 or 60 fps.
  • Background: the colour shown where no clip covers the canvas.

Play, save and export

  1. Press Space to play and pause. See The player for all playback controls.
  2. Press Ctrl + S to save. Your project is stored in this browser – see Projects and storage.
  3. Click Export (or Ctrl + E), choose a preset and download your MP4 – see Exporting your video.
Tip
