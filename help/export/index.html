Export & projects

Exporting your video

Exporting renders your edit into an MP4 file you can upload anywhere. It happens on your computer, uses your graphics card's video encoder when available, and the result is checked before you download it.

Start an export

Click Export at the top right (or press Ctrl + E).

The summary shows the final size, codecs, duration and an estimated file size. Warnings appear if something needs your attention, for example missing media.

While it renders

The progress view shows the stage (preparing, audio, rendering & encoding, finishing, checking), percentage, frame count, elapsed time and time remaining. Click Hide to keep working while it runs – the Export button shows the progress – or Cancel export to stop. Keep the tab open until it finishes.

Download

When the file is ready and verified, you can play it right in the dialog, see its size, duration, resolution and codecs, and click Download. Export again returns to the settings.

What is included

The export contains exactly what the player shows: all visible tracks, titles and captions, colour and effects, transitions, keyframe animation, speed changes, background removal and the mixed audio of all unmuted tracks. Hidden tracks are left out, muted tracks are silent and reversed clips are exported without sound.

Speed

On a typical computer with hardware encoding, 1080p renders several times faster than real time. 4K, many effects and background removal take longer.

The Project file (.json) download at the bottom of the settings saves your project's structure (not the media) as a backup.
