FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Vyora really free?

Yes. The editor, the AI tools and exporting are free, with no watermark and no account. The website is supported by advertising.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Vyora runs in a modern browser. We recommend the latest Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Your media is processed on your own device. The AI tools download their models once (from public model libraries) and then work offline.

Can it make captions in Urdu?

Yes – choose Urdu as the language in the Captions panel. For the best Urdu results pick the larger "Small" speech model. You can correct any word afterwards.

What can I export?

MP4 video with H.264 (or HEVC where your browser supports it) and AAC audio, from 480p up to 4K, at 24 to 60 frames per second.

Where is my project saved?

In your browser's storage on this computer. Save with Ctrl+S or turn on autosave. Clearing your browser data removes saved projects, so export your finished videos.
