How to add subtitles

Subtitles in four steps

  1. Open the editor and drop your video onto the timeline.
  2. Open the Captions panel and choose the language – or let Cutistic detect it – and a speech model (Base is a good start for English).
  3. Click Generate captions. The first time, the speech model downloads once; after that it works offline. The captions appear on their own track, timed word by word with the voice.
  4. Check and style them: fix any word, change font, size, colour, outline, background box and position for every caption at once. Then export your MP4 with the subtitles burned in, or click SRT (download) for a subtitle file.

Why add subtitles at all?

Many people watch videos on social media with the sound off – subtitles keep them watching. Captions also make your videos accessible to viewers who are deaf or hard of hearing, help people who speak another language follow along, and give search engines the words of your video.

FAQ

Questions about subtitles

Is the subtitle generator free?

Yes. Generating, editing, styling and exporting subtitles is free, with no limit on minutes and no watermark on the video.

Is my video uploaded to transcribe it?

No. The speech model is downloaded once and runs on your own computer. Your audio never leaves your device.

How accurate are the subtitles?

For clear English speech they are usually word-perfect. Background music, several people talking at once, strong accents and less common languages reduce accuracy – so always read through the captions before you publish.

Can I upload the subtitles to YouTube?

Yes. Click SRT (download) in the Captions panel and upload the .srt file in YouTube Studio under Subtitles. You can also burn the captions into the video itself.

The subtitles are a little early or late – what can I do?

Use Sync −0.1 s or +0.1 s to shift all captions together, or change the start and end time of a single caption in the list.
