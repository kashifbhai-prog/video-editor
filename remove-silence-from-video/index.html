How to

Cut every pause in four steps

  1. Open the editor and put your recording on the timeline.
  2. Open AI tools › Silence removal and click Detect silence.
  3. Review the pauses: they appear as red bands on the timeline and in a list. Untick any pause you want to keep (it turns grey), or click a time to jump there and listen.
  4. Click Remove … pauses. The gaps close on all unlocked tracks at once – and if you change your mind, it is a single undo.

FAQ

Questions about silence removal

Is silence removal free?

Yes. Detecting and removing pauses is free, with no limit on the length of your video and no watermark.

Will my music and captions go out of sync?

No. The pauses are cut from all unlocked tracks at the same time and the gaps close, so the picture, the voice, the music and the captions stay together. Lock a track if it should not be cut.

Can I keep some of the pauses?

Yes. Every detected pause is shown before anything is cut. Untick a pause in the list, or click the top of its band on the timeline, to keep it.

Is my video uploaded?

No. The audio is analysed on your own computer inside the browser.
