Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

In short

Your videos, photos, audio and projects are processed and stored in your own browser. We never receive them. We use cookies only for traffic statistics and advertising, and only with your consent where the law requires it.

Who we are

This website and the Cutistic video editor ("Cutistic", "we", "us") are operated by the Cutistic team. You can reach us at {{email}}.

Your media and projects

Cutistic is a browser application. When you import files, edit, use the AI tools or export a video, all processing happens on your device. Your media files, projects, captions and exported videos are not uploaded to us or to any other server.

To let you continue later, the editor stores your project and imported media in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB and localStorage) on your computer. You can delete it at any time by starting a new project in the editor's Settings or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

AI model downloads

The first time you use an AI feature, your browser downloads the required model files directly from public model libraries: Hugging Face (huggingface.co, speech recognition), jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net, runtime files) and Google (storage.googleapis.com, background removal). Like any website request, these providers receive your IP address and browser information when the files are downloaded. No video, audio or text from your project is sent to them. The files are then kept in your browser's cache.

Fonts

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com), which receives your IP address when the fonts are requested.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

We use the following third-party services, which may set cookies or use similar technologies:

  • Google Analytics – to understand how many people visit, which pages and editor features are used, and how to improve them. We send anonymous usage events (for example "export completed" with the chosen resolution), never the content of your projects or file names.
  • Google AdSense – to show advertising on our website pages (not inside the editor). Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.

Visitors are asked for consent before these cookies are used. If you reject, analytics and advertising cookies are not set; ads may still be shown without personalisation. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings in the footer.

You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, opt out of personalised advertising at adssettings.google.com, and install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account and do not collect your name, email or payment details to use Cutistic.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not access the files on your computer except those you choose to import.

Emails and feedback you send us

If you contact us by email, we use your message and email address only to reply and keep a record of the conversation.

The Feedback button in the editor sends us only what you type (the type of feedback, your message and, if you choose, your name and email address). If "Include technical info" is ticked, it also sends your browser and operating system name, screen and window size, language, the number of processor cores and whether your browser supports WebGL2, WebGPU and WebCodecs – you can see the exact details in the dialog before sending. Your videos and projects are never sent. We use feedback only to fix problems and improve Cutistic, and to reply when you leave an email address.

Children

Cutistic is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, object to processing and withdraw consent. Because we do not store your media or accounts, most data stays under your control in your browser. For anything else, write to {{email}}.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service evolves. The date at the top shows the latest version.
